# Chemical Bond - Slides 026-030

## Slide 26

### $sp^3d$ hybridization
Formation of five hybrid orbitals from one 3s, three 3p, and one 3d orbital.

### Diagram Analysis: Phosphorus Pentafluoride ($PF_5$)
*   **Phosphorus (P) Electronic States:**
    *   **Ground State ($3s^2 p^3$):** Filled 3s, three half-filled 3p, empty 3d.
    *   **Excited State ($3s^1 p^3 d^1$):** One electron promoted from 3s to 3d.
    *   **Hybridized State:** Five equivalent $sp^3d$ orbitals formed.
*   **Molecular Geometry (Trigonal Bipyramidal):**
    *   Three **Equatorial** F atoms: Form a triangle in the central plane. Bond angle = **$120^\circ$**. Bond length = **$1.53 \text{\AA}$**.
    *   Two **Axial** F atoms: Located above and below the plane. Bond angle to equatorial plane = **$90^\circ$**. Bond length = **$1.58 \text{\AA}$**.
*   **Key Detail:** Axial bonds are slightly longer and weaker than equatorial bonds due to greater repulsion.

## Slide 27

### $sp^3d$ hybridization: Characteristics
Combination of one s-, three p- and $d_{z^2}$- atomic orbitals gives five **trigonal bipyramidal** hybrid orbitals.

### The Two Sets of Hybrids:
1.  **Equatorial Hybrids:**
    *   Three equivalent hybrids directed toward the corners of a triangle.
    *   Planar arrangement with **$120^\circ$** angles.
    *   Formed by $s, p_x$, and $p_y$ orbitals.
2.  **Axial (or Polar) Hybrids:**
    *   Two equivalent hybrids oriented perpendicular to the equatorial plane.
    *   Angle between axial hybrids = **$180^\circ$**.
    *   Angle with equatorial plane = **$90^\circ$**.
    *   Formed by $p_z$ and $d_{z^2}$ orbitals.

**Examples:** $PF_5, :SF_4, ::ClF_3$, etc.

## Slide 28 & 29

### $sp^3d^2$ hybridization
Combination of $s, p_x, p_y, p_z, d_{z^2}$, and $d_{x^2-y^2}$ orbitals to produce six equivalent hybrid orbitals.

### Characteristics:
*   **Geometry:** **Octahedral** (hybrids directed toward the corners of a regular octahedron).
*   **Bond Angle:** **$90^\circ$** between all pairs.

### Diagram Analysis: Sulfur Hexafluoride ($SF_6$)
*   **Visual:** Shows a central Sulfur (S) atom with six Fluorine (F) atoms at the vertices.
*   **Configuration:** $bp's = 6, lp = 0$. All six S-F $\sigma$ bonds are equivalent.

**Examples:** $SF_6, AlF_6^{3-}, PF_6^-$ etc.

## Slide 30

### $dsp^2$ hybridization and Square Planar Geometry
Analysis of the Tetraamminecopper(II) complex: $[Cu(NH_3)_4]^{2+}$.

### Diagram Analysis: Electronic Configuration
*   **$Cu$ (Ground State):** $[Ar] 3d^{10} 4s^1$.
*   **$Cu^{2+}$ (Ion State):** $[Ar] 3d^9$. Has one unpaired electron in the 3d subshell.
*   **$dsp^2$ Hybridization Process:**
    *   One 3d orbital, one 4s, and two 4p orbitals are mixed.
    *   **Unpaired Electron Promotion:** The single unpaired electron from the 3d orbital is promoted to the vacant 4p orbital (specifically the third p-orbital).
*   **Coordination:** Four pairs of electrons from the $:NH_3$ ligands enter the newly formed $dsp^2$ hybrid orbitals.
*   **Resulting Geometry:** **Square Planar**.
