# Atomic Structure: Slides 061-065

## Slide 61

### **Significance of Quantum Numbers**

**Significance of quantum numbers**

- **Principal quantum number:** The size and energy of the orbit are defined by this quantum number.
- **Azimuthal quantum number:** The shape of the orbitals is defined by this quantum number.
- **Magnetic quantum number:** The spatial orientation of the orbital is determined by this quantum number.
- **Spin quantum number:** The spin of the electron is indicated by this quantum number.

---

## Slide 62

### **Conclusion for Quantum Number**

According to **Pauli’s exclusion principle**, no two electrons in the same atom can have the values of all quantum numbers identical. It means two electrons in the same orbitals must have opposite spins.

- The principal quantum number ‘n’ is associated with the energy and size of orbitals and has integral values i.e. n = 1, 2, 3, …
- The azimuthal quantum number ($\ell$) associates with the shape of orbitals and has the values of 0, 1, 2, and 3. The alphabets assigned for these azimuthal quantum numbers are s, p, d, and f respectively.
- The magnetic quantum number is associated with energy levels within a subshell and has different values related to the azimuthal quantum number by the rule ($2\ell+1$).
- The spin quantum number is associated with the orientation of electrons in an orbital and only has two values, $+1/2$, and $-1/2$.
