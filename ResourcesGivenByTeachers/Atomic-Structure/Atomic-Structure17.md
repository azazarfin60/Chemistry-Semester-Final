# Atomic Structure: Slides 081-085

## Slide 81

### **Three Rules of Filling Electrons**

Electrons are filled in the orbitals by following three rules:

- **Aufbau rule or ( n+l ) rule:** It states that the filling of electrons in orbital takes place in increasing order of their energies means lower energy orbital would fill first.
- **Hund’s Rule of maximum multiplicity:** It states that no pairing of electrons will occur until all the orbitals of a subshell are singly electron occupied and all the electrons have the same spin either all are clockwise or anticlockwise.
- **Pauli’s Exclusion Principle:** It states that an orbital can accommodate a maximum of 2 electrons with opposite spins only.

## Slide 82

### **Aufbau Principle**

“Electrons in different orbitals are filled in the increasing order of their energy, i.e. the orbital with the lowest energy will be filled first and the orbital with the highest energy will be filled last.”

> 📷 **[Diagram]** Aufbau Diagonal Rule Diagram

**Diagram Analysis:**
The slide presents the **Möller diagram** (diagonal rule) for determining the order of orbital filling:
- **Structure:** Orbitals are arranged in rows by principal quantum number ($n=1$ to $n=8$) and columns by orbital type.
- **Orbital Types:**
    - **Orange Spheres:** s-orbitals (1s to 8s)
    - **Purple Spheres:** p-orbitals (2p to 7p)
    - **Red Spheres:** d-orbitals (3d to 6d)
    - **Green Spheres:** f-orbitals (4f to 5f)
- **Filling Path:** Diagonal arrows trace the path of increasing energy, starting from 1s, then 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, etc.

## Slide 83

### **Aufbau Principle**

**Aufbau Principle Rules**

> 📷 **[Diagram]** Aufbau Principle Rules Visualization

**Diagram Analysis:**
The slide provides visual examples of correct and incorrect electron filling:

1. **Fill orbitals by energy:** An energy axis shows the progression: $1s < 2s < 2p < 3s < 3p < 4s < 3d < 4p < 4d < 4f$.
2. **Up to two electrons per orbital:**
   - **Correct (✔️):** Single electron in 1s.
   - **Incorrect (❌):** Two electrons with the same spin in 1s.
   - **Incorrect (❌):** Three electrons in 1s.
3. **Filling degenerate orbitals:**
   - **Correct (✔️):** Three electrons singly occupying three 2p orbitals with the same spin.
   - **Correct (✔️):** Four electrons, with one orbital paired and two singly occupied.
   - **Incorrect (❌):** Three electrons with one paired orbital and one empty orbital (violates Hund's rule).

## Slide 84

### **Aufbau Principle**

| Type of Orbital | Value of n | Value of l | Value of n+l | Relative Energy |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1s | 1 | 0 | 1+0 = 1 | Lowest energy |
| 2s | 2 | 0 | 2+0 = 2 | Higher energy than 1s orbital |
| 2p | 2 | 1 | 2+1 = 3 | 2p orbitals(n=2) have lower energy than 3s(n=3) orbital. |
| 3s | 3 | 0 | 3+0 = 3 | |
| 3p | 3 | 1 | 3+1 = 4 | 3p orbitals(n=3) have lower energy than 4s(n=4) orbitals. |
| 4s | 4 | 0 | 4+0 = 4 | |
| 3d | 3 | 2 | 3+2 = 5 | 3d orbitals(n=3) have lower energy than 4p(n=4) orbitals. |
| 4p | 4 | 1 | 4+1 = 5 | |

From the description given in the table, it follows that the energies of the various orbitals increase with the increase in the number of the principal quantum number.

- Thus the order in which the electrons are filled in the atomic orbital of the atom is, **1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s, 4d, 5p, 6s, 4f, 5d, 6p, 7s, 5f, 6d, 7p...**

## Slide 85

### **Limitation of Aufbau Principle**

- The Aufbau principle cannot be applied to predict electron configuration of atoms on ionization.
- The actual electronic configuration of copper and sulphur is not equal to the electronic configuration according to the principle.
- The Aufbau Principle is extremely effective for the ground state of the atoms up to the first 18 elements, but it is less effective for the remaining 100 electrons after that.
- Similar to the d and f block elements, which add stability to the atoms whether they are filled or partially filled, do not always follow the Aufbau Principle.
- When an ion is to be formed, Aufbau principle doesn’t tell which electrons are to be removed.
