## Slide 16
> 📷 **[Diagram]** Chemical Equilibrium-016

### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**

**SAMPLE EXERCISE 15.1 Writing Equilibrium-Constant Expressions**
Write the equilibrium expression for $K_c$ for the following reactions:
(a) $2 O_3(g) \rightleftharpoons 3 O_2(g)$
(b) $2 NO(g) + Cl_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 NOCl(g)$
(c) $Ag^+(aq) + 2 NH_3(aq) \rightleftharpoons Ag(NH_3)_2^+(aq)$

**Solution**
**Analyze**: We are given three equations and are asked to write an equilibrium-constant expression for each.
**Plan**: Using the law of mass action, we write each expression as a quotient having the product concentration terms in the numerator and the reactant concentration terms in the denominator. Each term is raised to the power of its coefficient in the balanced chemical equation.
**Solve**:
(a) $K_c = \frac{[O_2]^3}{[O_3]^2}$
(b) $K_c = \frac{[NOCl]^2}{[NO]^2[Cl_2]}$
(c) $K_c = \frac{[Ag(NH_3)_2^+]}{[Ag^+][NH_3]^2}$

**PRACTICE EXERCISE**
Write the equilibrium-constant expression, $K_c$ for:
(a) $H_2(g) + I_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 HI(g)$
(b) $Cd^{2+}(aq) + 4 Br^-(aq) \rightleftharpoons CdBr_4^{2-}(aq)$

**Answers**:
(a) $K_c = \frac{[HI]^2}{[H_2][I_2]}$
(b) $K_c = \frac{[CdBr_4^{2-}]}{[Cd^{2+}][Br^-]^4}$

## Slide 17
> 📷 **[Diagram]** Chemical Equilibrium-017

### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**
**Expressions for $K_c$ & $K_p$ in terms of the amount of a substance reacted or formed**

(a) Reaction: $N_2(g) + 3 H_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 NH_3(g)$

| Time | $N_2$ | $H_2$ | $NH_3$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Initially, t = 0 | 1 mol/L | 3 M | 0 |
| At eqlbm, x mol/L | (1-x) M | (3-3x) M | 2x M |

$$Kc = \frac{[NH_3]^2}{[N_2][H_2]^3} = \frac{(2x)^2}{(1-x)(3-3x)^3}$$

**In terms of pressure,**
Total no. of moles at equilibrium = $(1-x) + (3-3x) + 2x = 4-2x = 2(2-x)$

If $P$ is the total pressure of the equilibrium reaction mixture,
- $p_{N_2} = \frac{(1-x)P}{2(2-x)}$
- $p_{H_2} = \frac{3(1-x)P}{2(2-x)}$
- $p_{NH_3} = \frac{2xP}{2(2-x)}$

Substituting in the expression,
$$K_p = \frac{p_{NH_3}^2}{p_{N_2} p_{H_2}^3}$$

## Slide 18
> 📷 **[Diagram]** Chemical Equilibrium-018

### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**
**(b) Expression for $K_c$ & $K_p$ in terms of degree of dissociation ($\alpha$)**

Reaction: $2 HI(g) \rightleftharpoons H_2(g) + I_2(g)$

| Status | $HI$ | $H_2$ | $I_2$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Initially, no. of moles | $a$ | 0 | 0 |
| At eqlbm. No. of moles | $a(1-\alpha)$ | $a\alpha/2$ | $a\alpha/2$ |
| Molar conc. in V L | $\frac{a(1-\alpha)}{V}$ | $\frac{a\alpha}{2V}$ | $\frac{a\alpha}{2V}$ |

$$K_c = \frac{[H_2][I_2]}{[HI]^2} = \frac{(\frac{a\alpha}{2V})(\frac{a\alpha}{2V})}{[\frac{a(1-\alpha)}{V}]^2}$$
$$K_c = \frac{\alpha^2}{4(1-\alpha)^2}$$

**Expression for $K_p$**: no. of moles of reactants & products are equal, $n_r = n_p$ ($\Delta n = 0$)
$$K_c = K_p = \frac{\alpha^2}{4(1-\alpha)^2}$$

## Slide 19
> 📷 **[Diagram]** Chemical Equilibrium-019

### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**
**(c) Expression for $K_c$ & $K_p$ in terms of degree of dissociation ($\alpha$) for "dissociation of $PCl_5$"**

**Expression for $K_c$:**
Reaction: $PCl_5 (g) \rightleftharpoons PCl_3(g) + Cl_2 (g)$

- Initial no. of moles: $a, 0, 0$
- Moles at equilibrium: $a(1-\alpha), a\alpha, a\alpha$
- Molar concentration: $\frac{a(1-\alpha)}{V}, \frac{a\alpha}{V}, \frac{a\alpha}{V}$

$$K_c = \frac{[PCl_3][Cl_2]}{[PCl_5]} = \frac{(\frac{a\alpha}{V})(\frac{a\alpha}{V})}{\frac{a(1-\alpha)}{V}}$$
$$K_c = \frac{a\alpha^2}{(1-\alpha)V}$$

## Slide 20
> 📷 **[Diagram]** Chemical Equilibrium-020

### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**
**Expression for $K_p$ for : $PCl_5 (g) \rightleftharpoons PCl_3(g) + Cl_2 (g)$**

Total no. of moles at equilibrium $= a(1-\alpha) + a\alpha + a\alpha = a(1+\alpha)$
Let $P$ be the total pressure at equilibrium, then:
- $p_{PCl_5} = \frac{a(1-\alpha)P}{a(1+\alpha)} = \frac{(1-\alpha)P}{(1+\alpha)}$
- $p_{Cl_2} = \frac{(a.\alpha)P}{a(1+\alpha)} = \frac{\alpha P}{(1+\alpha)} = p_{PCl_3}$

$$K_p = \frac{p_{PCl_3} \times p_{Cl_2}}{p_{PCl_5}} = \frac{\frac{\alpha P}{(1+\alpha)} \times \frac{\alpha P}{(1+\alpha)}}{\frac{(1-\alpha)P}{(1+\alpha)}}$$
$$K_p = \frac{\alpha^2}{(1-\alpha)(1+\alpha)} P = \frac{\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2} P$$
