### Text Content
**Equilibrium Constant Expression**

Reaction: $N_2O_4(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 NO_2(g)$

**Expression for $K_c$:**
- Initial moles: $a, 0$
- Moles at equilibrium: $a(1-\alpha), 2a\alpha$ mol
- Molar conc. in V L: $a(1-\alpha)/V, 2a\alpha/V$ mol/L

$$K_c = \frac{[NO_2]^2}{[N_2O_4]} = \frac{(2a\alpha/V)^2}{\frac{a(1-\alpha)}{V}}$$
$$K_c = \frac{4a\alpha^2}{(1-\alpha)V}$$

**Expression for $K_p$:**
Total no. of moles at equilibrium $= a(1-\alpha) + 2a\alpha = a(1+\alpha)$
Let $P$ be the total pressure at equilibrium, then:
- $p_{N_2O_4} = \frac{a(1-\alpha)P}{a(1+\alpha)} = \frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha} P$
- $p_{NO_2} = \frac{2a\alpha P}{a(1+\alpha)} = \frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha} P$

$$K_p = \frac{p_{NO_2}^2}{p_{N_2O_4}} = \frac{[\frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha} P]^2}{\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha} P}$$
$$K_p = \frac{4\alpha^2}{(1-\alpha)(1+\alpha)} P = \frac{4\alpha^2}{1-\alpha^2} P$$

### Text Content
**Numerical Problem**

Hydrogen is produced industrially by the steam–hydrocarbon re-forming process. The reaction that takes place in the first step of this process is
$$H_2O(g) + CH_4(g) \rightleftharpoons CO(g) + 3 H_2(g)$$

(a) If $K_c = 3.8 \times 10^{-3}$ at 1000 K, what is the value of $K_p$ at the same temperature?
(b) If $K_p = 6.1 \times 10^4$ at $1125 ^\circ C$, what is the value of $K_c$ at $1125 ^\circ C$?

**STRATEGY**
To calculate $K_p$ from $K_c$, or vice versa, use the equation $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$, where $R = 0.08206 \text{ L}\cdot\text{atm/K}\cdot\text{mol}$, $T$ is in Kelvin, and $\Delta n = \text{moles gas products} - \text{moles gas reactants}$.

**SOLUTION**
(a) For this reaction, $\Delta n = (1 + 3) - (1 + 1) = 2$. Therefore,
$$K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n} = K_c(RT)^2 = (3.8 \times 10^{-3})[(0.08206)(1000)]^2 = 26$$

(b) Solving the equation $K_p = K_c(RT)^2$ for $K_c$ gives:
$$K_c = \frac{K_p}{(RT)^2} = \frac{6.1 \times 10^4}{[(0.08206)(1398)]^2} = 4.6$$
*Note: $1125 ^\circ C$ corresponds to $1125 + 273 = 1398$ K.*

### Text Content
**Numerical Problem**

The following equilibrium process has been studied at $230 ^\circ C$:
$$2 NO(g) + O_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 NO_2(g)$$

In one experiment the concentrations of the reacting species at equilibrium are found to be $[NO] = 0.0542 M, [O_2] = 0.127 M, \text{ and } [NO_2] = 15.5 M$. Calculate the equilibrium constant ($K_c$) of the reaction at this temperature.

**Strategy:** The concentrations given are equilibrium concentrations. Calculate $K_c$ using the law of mass action.

**Solution:** The equilibrium constant is given by:
$$K_c = \frac{[NO_2]^2}{[NO]^2[O_2]}$$
Substituting the concentrations:
$$K_c = \frac{(15.5)^2}{(0.0542)^2(0.127)} = 6.44 \times 10^5$$

### Text Content
**Numerical Problem**

The equilibrium constant ($K_c$) for the reaction
$$N_2O_4(g) \rightleftharpoons 2 NO_2(g)$$
is $4.63 \times 10^{-3}$ at $25 ^\circ C$. What is the value of $K_p$ at this temperature?

**Strategy:** Use $K_p = K_c(0.0821T)^{\Delta n}$.
$\Delta n = \text{moles gas products} - \text{moles gas reactants}$.

**Solution:**
Because $T = 298 \text{ K}$ and $\Delta n = 2 - 1 = 1$:
$$K_p = (4.63 \times 10^{-3})(0.0821 \times 298) = 0.113$$

### Text Content
**Numerical Problem - Example 14.5**

Write the equilibrium constant expression $K_c$, and $K_p$ if applicable, for each of the following heterogeneous systems:
(a) $(NH_4)_2Se(s) \rightleftharpoons 2 NH_3(g) + H_2Se(g)$
(b) $AgCl(s) \rightleftharpoons Ag^+(aq) + Cl^-(aq)$
(c) $P_4(s) + 6 Cl_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 4 PCl_3(l)$

**Strategy:** Concentrations of solids and liquids do not appear in the equilibrium constant expressions for heterogeneous systems.

**Solution:**
(a) Initially: $K'_c = \frac{[NH_3]^2[H_2Se]}{[(NH_4)_2Se]}$
Since $(NH_4)_2Se$ is a solid:
$$K_c = [NH_3]^2[H_2Se]$$
$$K_p = P_{NH_3}^2 P_{H_2Se}$$

(b) Initially: $K'_c = \frac{[Ag^+][Cl^-]}{[AgCl]}$
Since $AgCl$ is a solid:
$$K_c = [Ag^+][Cl^-]$$

(c) Initially: $K'_c = \frac{[PCl_3]^4}{[P_4][Cl_2]^6}$
Since $PCl_3$ is a liquid and $P_4$ is a solid:
$$K_c = \frac{1}{[Cl_2]^6}$$
$$K_p = \frac{1}{P_{Cl_2}^6}$$
