### **Rate of Reaction**

**For complex Reactions**

$$aA + bB + cC + \dots \rightarrow eE + fF + gG + \dots$$

where $a, b, c, \dots, e, f, g, \dots$ are **stoichiometric coefficients** in the balanced chemical equation & $A, B, C, \dots, E, F, G, \dots$ are **Chemical Species**

At const. V .....

$$\text{Rate} = -\frac{1}{a} \frac{\Delta [A]}{\Delta t} = -\frac{1}{b} \frac{\Delta [B]}{\Delta t} = \frac{1}{e} \frac{\Delta [E]}{\Delta t} = \frac{1}{f} \frac{\Delta [F]}{\Delta t}$$

> **Unit Of Rate:**
> **$\text{mol L}^{-1} \text{s}^{-1}$** for gaseous reactants & products, conc. is usually expressed as partial pressures, so $R$ is **$\text{atm s}^{-1}$**

### **Rate of Reaction**

#### **Difference between Rate of Reaction and Rate Constant**

| S.No. | Rate of reaction | Reaction rate constant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | It is speed with which reactants are converted into products. | It is proportionally constant. |
| 2 | It is measured as the rate of decrease of concentration of reactants or the rate of increase of concentration of products with time. | It is equal to the rate of reaction when the concentration of each of the reactants in unity. |
| 3 | It depends upon the initial concentration of reactants | It is independent of the initial concentration of the reactants. It has a constant value at fixed temperature. |

**Rate law:** The rate law for a chemical reaction is an expression that relates the reaction rate with the concentration of the reactant.

**Example:**
$$H_2 + I_2 \rightarrow 2 HI ; \quad \text{Rate} = k [H_2] \times [I_2]$$

## Slide 8

![Rate Law and Units of k](file:///home/azaz/Downloads/Documents/Chemical-Kinetics/Chemical-Kinetics-008.png)

### **Rate of Reaction**

| Rate Law | Order | Units of $k$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\text{Rate} = k$ | Zero | $\text{M s}^{-1}$ |
| $\text{Rate} = k[A]$ | First order with respect to A<br>First order overall | $\text{s}^{-1}$ |
| $\text{Rate} = k[A]^2$ | Second order with respect to A<br>Second order overall | $\text{M}^{-1} \text{s}^{-1}$ |
| $\text{Rate} = k[A][B]$ | First order with respect to A<br>First order with respect to B<br>Second order overall | $\text{M}^{-1} \text{s}^{-1}$ |
| $\text{Rate} = k[A][B][C]$ | First order with respect to A<br>First order with respect to B<br>First order with respect to C<br>Third order overall | $\text{M}^{-2} \text{s}^{-1}$ |

*In the units of $k$, M represents $\text{mol L}^{-1}$ or moles per liter.*
