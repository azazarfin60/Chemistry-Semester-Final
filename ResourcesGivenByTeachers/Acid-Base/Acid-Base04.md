# Acid-Base Lecture Notes (Continued)

## Slide 16: Practice Problems and Buffer Solutions

1. **Calculating the $pH$ of a Strong Base:** Determine the $pH$ of a $0.10\text{ M } NaOH$ solution.

2. **Calculating $pH$ of a Weak Acid:** Calculate the $pH$ of $0.1\text{ M } CH_3COOH$. The dissociation constant of acetic acid is $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$.

3. **Weak Acid Ionization:** Find the $pH$ of a $0.002\text{ M}$ acetic acid solution if it is $2.3\%$ ionized at this dilution.

4. **Diprotic Acid $pH$:** Calculate the $pH$ of $0.100\text{ M } H_2SO_4$ at $25^\circ C$. $K_{a1}$ is large and $K_{a2} = 0.012\text{ M}$.

5. **Buffer Solution Definition:** Define **BUFFER SOLUTION** and its types.

6. **Buffer Operation:** Explain **HOW A BUFFER OPERATES?**

7. **Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation:** **CALCULATION OF THE $pH$ OF BUFFER SOLUTIONS** (including the derivation of the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation).

8. **Buffer $pH$ Calculation 1:** Find the $pH$ of a buffer solution containing $0.20$ mole per liter $CH_3COONa$ and $0.15$ mole per liter $CH_3COOH$. $K_a$ for acetic acid is $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$.

9. **Buffer $pH$ Calculation 2:** A buffer solution contains $0.25\text{ M } NH_3$ and $0.40\text{ M } NH_4Cl$. Calculate the $pH$ of the solution. $K_b$ for ammonia = $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$.

10. **Effect of Acid Addition on Buffer:** The $pH$ of a buffer solution containing $0.5$ mole/liter $CH_3COOH$ and $0.5$ mole/liter $CH_3COONa$ is $4.76$. What will be the $pH$ of this solution after $0.1$ mole/liter $HCl$ has been added to the buffer? (Assume volume is unchanged, $K_a = 1.75 \times 10^{-5}$).

11. **Effect of Base Addition on Buffer:** A liter of solution containing $0.1$ mole of $CH_3COOH$ and $0.1$ mole of $CH_3COONa$ provides a buffer of $pH$ $4.74$. Calculate the $pH$ of the solution after the addition of $0.02$ mole $NaOH$. ($K_a = 1.8 \times 10^{-5}$).
