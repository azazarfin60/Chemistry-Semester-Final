**Course No: Chem-2117**  
**Course Title: Inorganic and Physical Chemistry**  
**Time: 3 Hours**  
**Full Marks: 72**

---

### Section - A

**1(a)** What is Chemical bond? Explain. **(04)**  
**1(b)** Compare the properties of ionic and covalent compounds. **(04)**  
**1(c)** Explain the following properties of metal on the basis of electron sea model: **(04)**  
i) Reflectivity  
ii) Malleability and Ductility

**2(a)** What is heat of reaction? Explain the effect of temperature on heat of reaction. **(05)**  
**2(b)** Derive $\Delta H = q_p$. Explain the exothermic and endothermic reaction with suitable examples. **(04)**  
**2(c)** Calculate the enthalpy of $C_2H_4(g) + H_2(g) \rightarrow C_2H_6(g)$ reaction at $25^\circ C$. The heat of combustion of ethylene, hydrogen and ethane are -1410 kJ, -286.2 kJ and -1560.6 kJ respectively at $25^\circ C$. **(03)**

**3(a)** What is periodic table? Why the long form of periodic Table is more clear than that of Mendeleef's periodic Table? **(06)**  
**3(b)** Explain the types of elements on the basis of electronic configuration. **(04)**  
**3(c)** What is successive ionization potential? **(02)**

**4** Write short notes on: **(12)**  
(a) Laws of Thermochemistry.  
(b) Electron affinity and Electronegativity.  
(c) Lewis concept of acids and bases.

**5(a)** Deduce an expression for the rate constant of a chemical reaction in which concentrations of two reactants are not identical. **(04)**
