# B.Sc. Engineering 2nd Year Odd Semester Examination 2018
**Course No: CHEM 2117**  
**Course Title: Inorganic and Physical Chemistry**  
**Time: 3 Hours**  
**Full Marks: 72**

---

### Section - A

**Q1** Short note: **(12)**  
(a) Schrodinger's wave equation  
(b) Magnetic quantum number  
(c) Hund's principle and $(n+l)$ rules

**Q2(a)** Define $\sigma$-bond and $\pi$-bond. Write down the theories of hybridization. Define $sp^3$ hybridization with $H_2O$, $NH_3$ and $CH_4$ molecules as example and also explain VSEPR theory. **(08)**  
**Q2(b)** Define "Band theory" of conductivity and explain metallic bond. **(04)**

**Q3(a)** For $aA + bB + \dots \rightleftharpoons mM + nN + \dots$ reaction, derive equations for $K_p$, $K_c$ and $K_x$ and also find relation among them. **(06)**  
**Q3(b)** For the following reaction $PCl_5 \rightleftharpoons PCl_3 + Cl_2$, derive equation for $K_p$ and $K_c$ and also find the effects of pressure and volume on it. **(06)**

**Q4(a)** Derive equation for $2^{\text{nd}}$ order reaction for both type $2A \rightarrow \text{product}$ and $A+B \rightarrow \text{product}$. Also find the properties of a $2^{\text{nd}}$ order reaction mathematically. **(08)**  
**Q4(b)** State and explain activated-complex theory with graphical representation. **(04)**

---

### Section - B

**Q5(a)** Define the rate of reaction, rate law, order of reaction and molecularity of reaction. **(04)**  
**Q5(b)** Differentiate between order of reaction and molecularity of reaction. **(04)**  
**Q5(c)** The half-life of a $1^{\text{st}}$ order reaction is 3400 min. Calculate the value of rate constant. What is the time required to complete 99% of the reaction? **(04)**

**Q6(a)** State and explain the law of mass action. **(03)**  
**Q6(b)** Derive the expression $\Delta G^\circ = -2.303 RT \log K_p$ and comment on it. **(05)**  
**Q6(c)** Calculate the value of $K_p$ for a reaction which has $\Delta G^\circ$ value -20 Kcal/mole at $25^\circ C$. **(04)**

**Q7(a)** Define electrolytes and classify them with examples. **(04)**  
**Q7(b)** Describe the mechanism of electrolytic conduction. **(04)**  
**Q7(c)** Define with examples the electrochemical cell and electrolytic cell. **(04)**

**Q8** Short note: (Any three) **(12)**  
(a) Crystal structure and isomorphism  
(b) Osmotic pressure of dilute solution  
(c) Buffer solution and Buffer's action  
(d) Velocity of ion and its experimental determination
